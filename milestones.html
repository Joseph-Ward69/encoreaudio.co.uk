Milestones

What's been built so far, newest first. For what's coming, see the roadmap.

  1. 21 Oct 2026

    Smarter sound settings and a self-updating library

    • Sound settings switch to your DAC or headphones the moment you plug them in
    • New albums appear on your phone by themselves, without a manual scan
    • The website gains HTTPS everywhere
  2. 20 Oct 2026

    Encore Audio is born, with DSD and its own decoder

    • The Encore Audio name, logo and licence agreement
    • DSD support: native to streamers that play it, converted with care for those that don't
    • Music in more than one folder, such as a separate DSD collection
    • Our own Apple Lossless decoder, proven bit-for-bit identical on 160 million samples
    • This website and the public beta
  3. 19 Oct 2026

    Help, gapless and Sonos

    • Built-in help, troubleshooting, and questions and answers
    • More reliable gapless playback on streamers
    • A warning when a VPN would stop phones reaching Encore from outside
    • Confirmed working on Sonos, WiiM and HiFi Rose
  4. 18 Oct 2026

    Never miss a new album

    • Scan for new music from the phone app, at home or away
    • Encore keeps watching your NAS through network blips and sleep
  5. 17 Oct 2026

    Your phone becomes a hi-fi player

    • Play on the phone itself through headphones, USB DACs and Bluetooth, including LDAC
    • EQ, tone controls and headphone correction profiles, measured for clean sound
    • Your whole library away from home, encrypted
    • Offline downloads at full quality
    • Colour themes, compilations and genres
  6. 14–16 Oct 2026

    A remote in your pocket

    • The Android app: browse, play and control your streamers
    • Playlists you can create and edit from the phone
    • WiiM streamers get their own play queue, so their app and remote work too
    • Internet radio, and continuous play with similar music
  7. 13 Oct 2026

    First music

    • Encore reads a whole NAS library: artwork, album details and biographies
    • Bit-perfect streaming to network streamers, with a signal-path view to prove it
    • Every track measured for dynamic range, loudness and genuine Hi-Res
