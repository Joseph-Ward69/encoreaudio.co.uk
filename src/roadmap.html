Roadmap

Where Encore Audio is heading. Timings are indicative: we'll release each stage as soon as it's ready, and some may come sooner. For what's already done, see the milestones.

  1. Now – end of 2026

    Beta for Windows and Android In progress

    Free while in beta, on as many streamers, phones and networks as possible.

    • "Send feedback" built into the PC program and the app
    • Notices when a new beta version is available
    • The Android app on Google Play's testing programme
    • Fixes and refinements from testers' feedback
  2. Early 2027

    iPhone and iPad app: beta

    The same app on Apple devices, shared through TestFlight.

    • Browse your library and control your streamers from an iPhone or iPad
    • Play on the device itself, with EQ, tone controls and headphone profiles
    • USB DACs, AirPlay and Bluetooth; lock screen and Control Centre
    • Offline downloads and listening away from home
  3. Q1 2027

    Full release for Windows and Android

    Encore Audio becomes a one-off purchase, with no subscription and a free trial. The phone and tablet apps stay free.

    • Encore Audio on Google Play
    • A signed Windows installer
    • A thank-you for beta testers
  4. Q2 2027

    iPhone and iPad app on the App Store

    Free, for everyone with Encore Audio at home.

  5. From mid-2027

    What we're looking at next

    • Play similar: one tap for endless music in the style of what's playing
    • Remote access with no router setup
    • Encore Audio for Mac, and for running on a NAS
    • Even volume across albums (ReplayGain) and crossfade
    • Separate sound settings per format, such as DSD
    • Hi-res streaming services alongside your own library
