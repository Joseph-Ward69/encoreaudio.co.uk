The beta

Encore Audio is in a small, free beta: 15 places, so every tester gets a proper hearing.

Applications are open. We're choosing a mix of streamers, phones, DACs and libraries, so tell us about your system.
Apply for the beta

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC on your home network, to run the Encore Audio server. It needs to be on while you listen.
  • Your music files on that PC or on a NAS (ALAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3 or DSD).
  • One or more network streamers (WiiM, HiFi Rose, Bluesound, Sonos or any DLNA/UPnP player), and/or an Android phone or tablet (Android 8 or later).

How it works

  1. Apply with the form: your streamers, phone, DAC and headphones, and roughly how big your library is.
  2. We choose 15 testers for a good spread of equipment, and reply to everyone who applies.
  3. You get an invitation email with a private download for Windows, your personal beta key, and an invitation to the Android app through Google Play.
  4. Listen, and tell us what you think. The beta runs until the full release, planned for early 2027, and testers get a thank-you when it launches.

Your beta key is personal to you. Please don't share it or the download; see the licence agreement.

Setting up (for testers)

  1. Install and start Encore Audio, and paste in your beta key. Allow it through Windows Firewall on private networks when asked.
  2. Choose your music folder in the welcome window. A big library takes a few minutes to read the first time.
  3. Pick a streamer in the player list at the bottom right of the window, open an album and press Play.
  4. On your phone: install the app from your Google Play invitation, then in Encore go to Settings → Phone and tablet → Pair a phone or tablet, and type the code into the app.
  5. Optional: listen away from home. Settings → Away from home explains how to open a port on your router. Help (the ? button) has step-by-step guides and troubleshooting.
Windows will warn you about the installer. This early version isn't yet signed with a code-signing certificate, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. The final release will be signed.

Sending feedback

Email contact@encoreaudio.co.uk. It helps a lot if you include:

  • which streamers, phone and DAC or headphones you use,
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened instead,
  • for a problem on the PC: Encore's log (Help → Open log), attached to the email,
  • for a sound problem on the phone: Sound → Output → Copy audio diagnostics, pasted into the email.

Known issues

  • There's no iPhone or iPad app yet; it's on the roadmap.
  • A VPN running on the Encore PC can stop streamers or phones away from home reaching it. Turn the VPN off, or add Encore.exe to its split tunnelling.
